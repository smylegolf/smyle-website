We're not your typical golf club. We're golf for people who never thought golf was for them.

How It Works Six steps.
Golf, your
way.

No membership needed to get started. But once you're in, every session goes a lot further.

01 Create your account

Sign up at albaplay.com in a minute. Free, no card, no commitment.

02 Join the club

Pick a membership to play with the crew, or keep it private for focused practice. Or just book a one-off at the standard rate.

03 Pick your date and time

Choose when you want to come in and how long you want to play. Instant availability, instant booking.

04 Bring the crew

Pricing is per bay. Bringing friends? Add extra players for just €5 per person per hour.

05 Need clubs?

Rent a full set for €5. Perfect if you're coming without gear or bringing someone for the first time.

06 Show up and play

Pay online, come in, and enjoy. We'll see you at Calle María Cubí 26. No dress code, no fuss.
