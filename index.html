FAQs Good
to know.

Everything you need before your first visit.

How does Smyle work?

Smyle is, first and foremost, a members' club. If you want to be part of it, check out our membership options here.

Not a member? No problem — you can book a private bay and come play with friends. That simple.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes — you can only play with a reservation. No booking, no golf. Book here.

Do you offer classes?

Yes. Relaxed, accessible coaching for all levels. We offer:

  • Quick Swing Fix — a fast, focused fix (€20)
  • Private Lesson — 1-on-1 with a PGA pro (€70)
  • On Course Lesson — play a virtual course with your coach (€80)
  • Kids Lesson — juniors welcome (€30)

Prefer to save? Check our value packs — up to 29% off.

Browse all classes →

Do I need to bring my own clubs?

You're welcome to bring your own, but you don't have to. We have free iron sets for Men (right & left-handed), Women (right-handed), and Kids (right-handed).

*We don't stock woods or drivers — bring your own if you want to use them.

I'm left-handed — can I play?

Yes. Bay 1 accommodates both right and left-handed players. Just let us know when booking so we can set up the space correctly.

What technology do you use?

We use Uneekor tracking systems with GSPro software — the same combo used by serious golfers and complete beginners alike. Real-time ball and swing data, incredible photo-realistic virtual courses. Every shot feels smooth, technical, and fun.

How long does it take to play 18 holes?

It depends on the group and experience level. Rough guide:

  • Solo: 45–60 minutes
  • Two players: 60–90 minutes
  • Group of 4: ~2 hours
  • Full bay (6 players): 2.5–3 hours

Running short on time? You can always play fewer holes.

I've never played golf — can I come?

Yes — beginners are always welcome at Smyle. We do ask that first-timers book a First Timer session before their first regular session. It's a quick way to learn the basics, pick up some golf etiquette, and make sure everyone plays safely and confidently.
